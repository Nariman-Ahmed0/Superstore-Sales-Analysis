# Superstore-Sales-Analysis
Sales analysis of the Superstore dataset: exploring sales, profit, categories, and regions to find key business insights.

## Objective
Analyze Superstore sales data to understand how sales are distributed across regions, categories, sub-categories, states, and time, and to identify what drives sales and what needs further analysis.

## Dataset
The dataset contains Superstore orders with sales values, plus customer, order, region, state, category, and sub-category details. It does not include Profit, Cost, Discount, or Quantity, so the analysis focuses on sales only.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX measures (Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, Avg Order Value, Avg Sales Per Customer)
- Data modeling (fact and dimension tables)

## Key Insights
- *Region:* West leads with $710,219.68 (31.40% of total sales). West and East together make up 61.01%, while South is the lowest at 17.21%.
- *Category:* Technology leads with $827,455.87 (36.59%), 17.30% higher than Office Supplies. Furniture and Office Supplies are close, with a gap of only 3.29%.
- *Sub-Category:* Phones ($327,782.45) and Chairs ($322,822.73) together make up 28.77% of total sales. The top 10 sub-categories account for 88.03%.
- *State:* California ($446,306.46) and New York ($306,361.15) together make up 33.28% of total sales.
- *Year:* Sales grew 20.30%, from $600,192.55 in 2017 to $722,052.02 in 2018.
- *Seasonality:* November and December generate almost 30% of annual sales (27.8% in 2018 and 29.1% in 2017).

## Data Limitations
The dataset has no Profit, Cost, Discount, or Quantity columns, so this project cannot show which category or region is the most profitable, or whether discounts reduce profit. These columns would be needed for a full profitability analysis.
